# DumEQ illustrations

The public site uses four optimised WebP assets. Generated PNG originals are kept locally in this directory. The source photographs are not part of the public site.

- `dumeq-mascot.webp`: welcome-screen mascot, 660 × 720.
- `open-door.webp`: questionnaire margin illustration, 500 × 600.
- `incident-pile.webp`: results illustration, 780 × 520.
- `hyungil-portrait.webp`: About-page portrait, 800 × 1000.

## Generation mode and prompts

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The first three illustrations used text-to-image generation. The portrait used multi-reference image editing with four user-supplied photographs. WebP copies were resized and encoded for the website using Sharp.

Shared art direction: loose black marker and coloured-pencil fills; imperfect hand-drawn editorial doodles resembling a photocopied school worksheet or a 1990s instruction manual; chunky uneven outlines and tactile crayon texture; mustard yellow, tomato red, cobalt blue, muted teal, black and off-white. No readable text, logos, watermarks, drug-use imagery, injury, glossy 3D or corporate vector styling.

### Welcome mascot

A friendly cartoon brain in an oversized office tie clutches a clipboard with blank checkboxes and marches confidently in the wrong direction, with an obviously open door behind it. Include a loose shoelace and a pencil tucked behind the brain like an ear. Full-body, three-quarter pose, slight tilt, compact silhouette, transparent background. The joke comes from the harmless situation.

### Open door

A cheerful office-worker mascot in a crooked paper hat holds a smartphone upside down while seriously trying to unlock an already-open door. A spare key rests visibly on the person's head. Isolated vertical cutout with a few motion lines and a transparent background; earnest and warm rather than demeaning.

### Incident evidence

A crooked clipboard surrounded by a tipped paper cup and harmless puddle, hidden keys, a clock with mismatched hands, an abandoned cardboard rocket, a sock, a traffic cone, a magnifying glass and speech bubbles containing tangled scribbles. A compact horizontal cutout on a transparent background; no people or injuries.

### Hyungil portrait

Use all four supplied photographs as references for the same adult man, preserving his recognisable facial proportions, long centre-parted black hair, soft smile, warm eyes and small round necklace. Depict only this adult man. Draw him navigating a small emerald-green boat made from folded research paper, a metaphor for exploring people's stories. His upper-body portrait dominates: a white tee, loose navy jacket, windswept hair, friendly smile, pencil and notebook. Surround him with a few floating pages, tangled-to-neat speech bubbles, a tiny friendly robot pencil-holder, golden leaves and blue waves. Use black ink contours and coloured-pencil shading on warm ivory paper, with the shared website palette. Portrait format 4:5, coherent single scene, no text or logos. Warm, thoughtful and a little absurd; retain likeness rather than exaggerating ethnic features.

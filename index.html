    What happens to your answers

    Who operates this site

    Hyungil Suh is the site operator and data controller for the optional community contribution. Contact: ilgyu86@gmail.com. This entertainment site does not represent or imply endorsement by the University of Exeter.

    What is processed

    The questionnaire runs in your browser. Until you choose to contribute, the 19 answers exist only in the current page and are cleared when you reload or close it. The site uses no answer cookies, local storage, advertising, behavioural analytics, account, demographic form, or free-text field.

    If you opt in, the 19 answer values are sent once to Supabase and immediately added to per-question aggregate counts. The DEQ database does not create or keep a row containing your individual response pattern. It does not receive your name, email, substance, dose, location, narrative, or exact event date.

    Why and on what basis

    The only purpose is to maintain anonymous community totals and improve this entertainment website. Where the brief transmission is personal data, the operator relies on your explicit, optional consent under Article 6(1)(a), and Article 9(2)(a) if an answer is treated as health-related information. Refusing does not affect access to your result.

    Hosting logs and international processing

    GitHub hosts the website and Supabase receives optional submissions. Like most hosting providers, they may automatically process IP address, browser information, request time, security cookies, and technical-log metadata. The DEQ application does not copy those identifiers into its response counts. Their own retention, security, and international-transfer arrangements are described in their privacy notices.

    Retention, withdrawal, and reuse

    You can decline or withdraw before pressing the contribution button. Once the counts have been incremented, there is no individual DEQ record or identifier that can be found, linked back, or removed. The totals will not be used as research data, in a paper, for marketing, or to train an AI system. A future research project would require a separate route and fresh information and consent.

    Your choices

    You may use the full questionnaire and see the result without contributing. Questions about privacy can be sent to the contact above. UK users may also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office, and EU users may contact their local data-protection authority.

    This notice explains the implemented data flow; it is not legal advice or a claim that consent removes all data-protection obligations.

    Hello!

    I'm Hyungil (Hugh).

    Interested in extraordinary experiences. And the very human things that happen along the way.

    I am a PhD researcher at the Exeter Psychedelic Interdisciplinary Centre (EPIC), within the Department of Psychology at the University of Exeter, supervised by Dr Leor Roseman.

    My research uses large language models to help make sense of large collections of first-hand psychedelic narratives. I am particularly interested in the social dimensions of psychedelics: the people, relationships and contexts around the experience. I'm one of three Korean researchers working in psychedelics.

    DumEQ leaves a little room for the less transcendent moments. Sometimes you discover the meaning of life. Sometimes you spend twenty minutes trying to unlock a door that's already open.

    Privacy notice · 17 September 2026 · v3

    What happens to your answers

    Who operates this site

    Hyungil Suh is the site operator and data controller for saved questionnaire responses. Contact: h.suh@exeter.ac.uk. This personal entertainment site does not represent or imply endorsement by the University of Exeter.

    What is processed

    We do not collect direct identifiers such as your name or email address through this questionnaire. There is no account, demographic form, free-text field, advertising or behavioural analytics. The application does not use cookies or browser storage to keep answers. If you contact us by email, your email address and message will of course be received to handle your request.

    Without saving consent, answers remain in the current page and disappear when it is closed or reloaded. If you choose “Agree & Begin Assessment”, pressing “Save & see results” sends all 19 numerical answers together to Supabase. We retain this response set, the questionnaire and consent versions, the consent/submission date (not the exact time), an expiry date, a random response ID and a one-way hash of your deletion code. No substance name, dose, location or event date is requested.

    Answers can reveal sensitive information about psychedelic experiences, health and behaviour. Removing names does not make a response necessarily anonymous: patterns could be combined with access logs or other information to identify someone. We treat saved responses as potentially identifiable and do not attempt to identify respondents or join their answers to access logs.

    Why and on what basis

    The purpose is to understand answer patterns and improve this entertainment website. We rely on your optional consent under UK/EU GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) for any health-related special-category information. Saving is enabled only when you choose “Agree & Begin Assessment”. You can withdraw saving consent before finishing via Privacy & choices and still see your result. Scores are calculated in your browser for entertainment; they are not used to make decisions about you.

    Hosting logs and international processing

    GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase stores saved responses in its London database region. Individual responses are accessible to the site operator and necessary hosting providers, not to other visitors or the public. GitHub and Supabase may process IP addresses, browser information, request times, security cookies and technical logs. The application does not copy those identifiers into the response table.

    A UK database region does not mean all support, logs, backups or subprocessors stay in the UK. Provider processing may involve other countries. Details of their retention, subprocessors and transfer safeguards are available in the GitHub privacy statement, Supabase privacy policy and Supabase data-processing terms. Contact the operator for questions about these arrangements.

    Retention, withdrawal, and reuse

    Change your saving choice at any time before the final button. After submission, use your deletion code below to withdraw consent and remove the response from the live database. Keep the code: without it we may be unable to locate your response. A hash of a used deletion code is retained for 12 months, without your answers, to prevent delayed retries from restoring them. Deletion does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Provider backups and logs follow their own expiry schedules and may not disappear immediately.

    Saved responses expire after 12 months and are removed by an automatic daily cleanup. They will not be used for academic research, a paper, marketing or AI training. Future research would need a separate route, appropriate review and fresh information and consent. Earlier versions of this site accepted aggregate-only contributions; those cannot be reconstructed as individual responses.

    Your choices

    You can withdraw saving consent for this session below before finishing, or delete a submitted response using its deletion code. To request access, correction, restriction, portability or help deleting a response, contact the operator, ideally with your deletion code. We only ask for information needed to handle the request. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or your local EU data-protection authority.

    Delete a saved response

    This removes the matching response from the live database. No name or email is needed.

    This notice explains the implemented data flow; it is not legal advice or a claim that consent removes all data-protection obligations.
